Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

MyAutoPoster ("the App") is a personal automation tool used to schedule and publish video content to the developer's own TikTok account. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Access

The App connects to the TikTok API using OAuth authorization. It accesses only the minimum information required to publish video content on behalf of the authorized account, including:

  • Basic account identifiers needed to post content
  • Video files explicitly provided by the account owner for publishing

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the TikTok API is used solely to publish video content to the connected TikTok account. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

The App does not maintain a public database of user information. Any access tokens or credentials are stored securely and used only for the purpose of automating content publishing for the account owner.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties, except as required to operate the core publishing functionality through TikTok's official API.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Access credentials are retained only as long as necessary to provide the automation service. The account owner may revoke access at any time through TikTok's app permissions settings, which will immediately stop any further data access.

6. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the TikTok Developer Portal.
